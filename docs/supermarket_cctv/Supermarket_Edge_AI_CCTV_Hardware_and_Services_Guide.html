1. Executive Summary & Deployment Objective

This technical document provides the complete hardware requirements, workload mathematical sizing, and feature validation guide to procure, install, and operate the on-premise Supermarket Edge AI CCTV Platform for a commercial retail supermarket with 30 or more concurrent cameras.

The system runs 100% on-premise inside the store to protect shopper privacy, decode camera sub-streams in dedicated hardware, track customer journeys and product shelf hand-reaches, correlate visual shopper interest with cash register (POS) receipts, and automatically synthesize daily actionable store layout, staffing, and pricing optimizations without cloud subscription fees.

2. System Workload Mathematics & Sizing Proofs (30–36 Cameras)

To guarantee continuous 24/7 reliability without video frame drops, buffer bloat, or GPU Out-of-Memory (OOM) faults, hardware specifications are calculated using empirical throughput models:

A. Video Ingestion & Network Bandwidth

Bitrate = 1.5 – 2.5 Mbps per 720p stream
Total Ingress = 32 × 2.0 Mbps = 64 Mbps (~8 MB/s)

Requirement: A standard 1 GbE or 2.5 GbE onboard network card operates at <7% capacity, ensuring negligible latency. Cameras must be placed on an isolated surveillance VLAN.

B. Multi-Model Inference Budget

AI FPS = 32 Cameras × 3.0 FPS = 96 FPS Total
Batch Compute Window = 1000ms / 96 = 10.4 ms

Performance Headroom: TensorRT 10.x running batched YOLOv11s on an RTX 4070 Ti Super completes in ~2.1 ms, leaving >75% GPU compute free for hand pose estimation and Re-ID vectors.

C. GPU Video Memory (VRAM) Allocation Model

V_total = V_frame_buffers (1.8 GB) + V_models_TensorRT (2.7 GB) + V_cuda_context (2.5 GB) + V_local_LLM (5.0 GB) = 12.0 GB Minimum
Subsystem Component Memory Footprint Technical Allocation Detail
32-Stream Video Frame Buffers 1.8 GB 32 streams × (1280×720×3 bytes FP16) × 4 batch queues in shared memory
TensorRT Multi-Model Engine 2.7 GB YOLOv11s (0.6 GB) + YOLO-Pose 17-pt (0.85 GB) + FastReID (0.9 GB) + Demographics (0.35 GB)
CUDA Ring Context & Inter-Process SHM 2.5 GB Pinned memory circular buffers, cuDNN caches, and Zero-Copy IPC pipes
Quantized Local LLM Reasoning Headroom 5.0 GB 4-bit quantized Qwen 2.5 7B / Llama 3.2 3B for offline daily decision generation
Total Peak VRAM Requirement 12.0 – 14.5 GB Mandates 16 GB VRAM GPU (12GB cards will crash under peak evening traffic)
Hardware Decoder Architecture & Single NVDEC Throughput: Decoding 32 concurrent RTSP video streams via software on the CPU consumes >90% of a 16-core processor. The RTX 4070 Ti Super (AD103) features 1x 5th-Gen NVDEC hardware decoder (paired with Dual 8th-Gen NVENC encoders). A single 5th-Gen NVDEC engine decodes over 3,800 FPS of 720p H.264/H.265 (>120 concurrent streams at 30 FPS). At 32 cameras × 3 FPS decimated = 96 FPS aggregate, the single NVDEC engine runs at <3% capacity (<25% load even at full 30 FPS).
CRITICAL PROCUREMENT WARNING (SUPER vs. NON-SUPER): Only procure the RTX 4070 Ti SUPER (16GB VRAM, AD103 die) or RTX 5070 Ti (16GB GDDR7, GB203 die). The baseline non-Super RTX 4070 Ti has only 12GB VRAM on a 192-bit bus and is strictly disqualified as 12GB will trigger fatal Out-of-Memory (OOM) crashes during peak traffic.

5. Peripherals, Power Protection & Supermarket LAN Topology

Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS)

Recommended: APC Smart-UPS 1000VA / 1500VA LCD ($320 – $450)
Supermarket compressors and refrigeration cycling cause transient voltage sags. A 1000VA UPS provides 35–50 minutes of emergency battery runtime during power brownouts. Enable Restore on AC Power Loss: Power ON in motherboard BIOS for automatic recovery.

Surveillance VLAN & Switch Topology

Recommended: Managed 32-Port PoE+ Gigabit Switch
Isolate all Dahua/ONVIF CCTV cameras onto a dedicated VLAN (192.168.20.0/24). The edge PC connects via its 2.5 GbE NIC to ingest camera streams, while a secondary NIC or store LAN port connects to POS cash registers, ensuring zero packet congestion on checkout lanes.

4. Retail Funnel Equations & Queue Telemetry

• Attraction Rate (α): $(N_{dwell} / N_{pass}) \times 100\%$
• Engagement Rate (β): $(N_{interact} / N_{dwell}) \times 100\%$
• True Visual Conversion (γ): $(N_{sales} / N_{interact}) \times 100\%$
• Lost Sales Friction Index (φ): $((N_{interact} - N_{sales}) / N_{interact}) \times 100\%$
• Real-time queue length, customer wait times, and service rate ($\mu$) across 6 checkout registers.

Ready in app/services/retail_analytics_service.py

7. Supermarket Procurement & Installation Checklist

  1. Procure Edge PC Hardware: Order custom components from Section 3 (or pre-built workstation from Section 4). Ensure GPU is 16GB RTX 4070 Ti Super or RTX 4080.
  2. Procure APC Smart-UPS 1000VA: Connect edge workstation power to battery-backed outlets.
  3. Configure Motherboard BIOS: Enable Restore on AC Power Loss: Power ON and Above 4G Decoding / Re-Size BAR.
  4. Install OS & Drivers: Ubuntu 24.04 LTS (x86_64), NVIDIA Driver 550.x+, CUDA 12.4, Docker + NVIDIA Container Toolkit.
  5. Network Cabling: Connect Edge PC 2.5 GbE port to the managed camera PoE switch (VLAN 192.168.20.0/24) and secondary port to the store router.
  6. Deploy & Calibrate: Run docker compose up -d, navigate to /dashboard/studio, map product shelf zones, and verify camera tracking.
